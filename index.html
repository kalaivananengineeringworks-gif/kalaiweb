Our Story

Built By Hand.
Trusted By Industry.

Kalai Engineering Works began in 2014 as a single forge and a small team of blacksmiths in Tamil Nadu. Every gate, railing and structural component that leaves our workshop is shaped the way our founders learned it — heated, hammered and finished by people who take the work personally.

Over a decade later, we've grown into a full-service forging, welding and fabrication house serving residential, commercial and industrial clients — without losing the hand-forged discipline we started with.

Mission

Deliver metalwork engineered to outlast the buildings it's installed in, without cutting a single corner on material or craft.

Vision

To be India's benchmark for precision forging — where "Kalai-made" means the job is done right, permanently.

A Decade, Forged
2014

Kalai Engineering Works founded as a small blacksmith forge, taking on custom gates and residential ironwork.

2016

2018

Opened a larger workshop with dedicated forging and machining bays to handle heavier commercial orders.

2020

Built out a custom engineering division for machine components and one-off industrial parts.

2023

Crossed 500 completed projects, with a growing base of repeat commercial and industrial clients.

Today

A full-service forging, welding and fabrication house — still hand-finishing every piece that leaves the shop.
